The **NIST framework** refers primarily to the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF), a voluntary set of guidelines from the ***National Institute of Standards and Technology*** for organizations to manage cybersecurity risk by providing a common language, categories for activities, and best practices to enhance cybersecurity outcomes. It is organized into six core functions: **Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover,** and **Govern**.
